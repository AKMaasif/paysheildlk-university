1About PayShield.lk

A SIM swap is the moment a fraudster gets a SIM card in a victim's name. Bank codes and alerts then go to the fraudster, and a payment looks like it came from the customer's own trusted phone.

The idea

Banks see the payment. Telecom operators see the SIM change. PayShield.lk reads both at the same time, holds the money, and asks the real customer on the phone and account they registered, not on the SIM a fraudster controls.

Two feeds, one viewA telecom agent reads SIM, handset, location and port-out signals. A banking agent reads payments and compares them with what is usual for that customer.
Rules decideA fixed rule layer holds, blocks or releases. A language model only writes the analyst's note. It never moves money.
The customer proves itA live face scan (straight, left, right) and security questions confirm it is the real person before a held payment is released.

Who uses it

PersonWhat they do
CustomerRegisters once on their phone, answers "Did you make this payment?", completes the face scan and security questions when asked, and receives a receipt.
Fraud analystWatches cases live, reviews held payments, reads incident reports and tunes the hold threshold.
Bank supervisorDecides held payments that the rules cannot settle and handles escalated cases.
Telecom operatorViews SIM events and the feed that PayShield.lk uses.
AuditorRead-only view of cases, reports and the audit log. Cannot run drills.
AdministratorManages users and settings.
Please readThis is a pilot built for study. Every person, balance and payment is fictional, and the telecom and bank feeds are simulated. Do not enter real personal or banking details.

2How a payment is protected

Every payment follows the same path. The rule layer makes the decision; the agents gather the evidence and keep the record.

1 Case opensSupervisor
2 Read feedsTelecom + Banking
3 ScoreRisk agent
4 DecidePolicy rules (no model)
5 AskAlert agent
6 RecordReport agent
DecisionWhenWhat happens next
BlockOpen port-out, password reset within 6 hours, new payee and Rs. 500,000 or more.Blocked at once. SIM freeze requested, account limited.
HoldRisk score of 45 or more, or a SIM swap in the last 72 hours with Rs. 50,000 or more and another warning sign.The customer is asked on the registered phone.
ReleaseEverything else.Released by the rules and recorded.

When a payment is held

  1. The customer is asked. "Did you make this payment?" appears in the app. The SMS and email are generic and carry no link and no code.
  2. "No, this was not me." The payment is blocked, a SIM freeze is requested and the account is limited.
  3. "Yes, this was me." The customer does the face scan, then answers rounds of security questions.
  4. A pass re-checks the signals one last time, then releases the money. A fake face, or failed answers, block the payment.
  5. No answer. After 3 minutes the customer is reminded. After 10 minutes a human analyst takes over and the money stays held.
Safe by designIf an agent crashes or both AI models are down, the rules still decide and the payment stays held rather than being released.

4Install and first start

From an empty folder to a running fraud desk in about ten minutes.

Download and start

# 1. Download the project
git clone https://github.com/AKMaasif/paysheildlk-university.git
cd paysheildlk-university/university-project

# 2. Create your settings file (Windows PowerShell: copy .env.example .env), then set JWT_SECRET, HMAC_KEY and ENCRYPTION_KEY (32+ characters) before real use
cp .env.example .env

# 3. Build and start everything (console and customer site at http://localhost:3000)
docker compose up --build

Set the three secrets

Open .env in a text editor and give each of these a long random value (at least 32 characters). Never share them or commit the file.

SettingUsed for
JWT_SECRETSigning sign-in tokens.
HMAC_KEYSigning the handoffs between agents.
ENCRYPTION_KEYEncrypting authenticator secrets, face data and customer details. If you lose it, stored data cannot be read. If you change it, start with a fresh database (docker compose down -v).

Check that it works

  1. Open http://localhost:8000/api/health. You should see "ok": true, the database as postgres and Redis as connected.
  2. Open http://localhost:3000 and sign in to the console: analyst@payshield.lk / pilot-analyst.
  3. Open http://localhost:3000/c for the customer site.
  4. API documentation is at http://localhost:8000/docs.
Demo passwordsThe console accounts and passwords above are for the pilot demonstration only. Change them before anything is shown outside the team.

5Customer guide (phone)

The customer site is built for phones. Open it at /c on your site address.

5.1 Register (once)

Registration proves you are the person on the bank's record. It has four steps.

  1. Your details. Full name, email, NIC, mobile number and bank account number, exactly as they appear in the bank's records. Anything that does not match is refused.
  2. Password. Choose a username and a strong password.
  3. Face scan. Allow the camera, then follow the on-screen arrows (see 5.2).
  4. Authenticator. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app and type the 6-digit code it shows.
Demo customersIn the console, Demo controls shows each demo customer's registration details, their security answers and the authenticator QR code, so you can register them quickly.

5.2 The face scan: straight, left, right

The face scan is the same simple three-step scan every time, at registration and again when you confirm a held payment.

1 · Look straightHold still for a moment after the 3-second countdown.
2 · Turn to your leftSlowly. An arrow on the picture shows the way. Then come back to the middle.
3 · Turn to your rightSlowly, then back to the middle.
For a good scan
  • Use good, even light on your face.
  • Keep your whole face in the oval.
  • Keep your eyes open and move only as far as is comfortable.
  • Do not use a photo or a screen. A still image fails the check.
If a scan is not acceptedYou are told what to improve and can try again. Poor light or framing does not count against you. A different face or a fake twice blocks the payment.

5.3 Sign in

Sign in with your username and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. The password alone never opens a session. Signing out ends the session straight away.

5.4 Your account

Your home screen shows your balance, account details and recent transactions. If a payment needs you, a highlighted card appears at the top with the question.

5.5 When a payment needs you

A card appears: "Did you make this payment?" with the amount and payee. The money stays held until you answer.

No, this was not meThe payment is blocked, a SIM freeze is requested and your account is limited. You will then be asked to choose a new password.
Yes, this was meDo the face scan (straight, left, right), then answer the security questions. A pass releases the payment.

Security questions

Each round asks three of your eight security questions, in a random order. You can have up to four rounds. We never say which answer was wrong.

5.6 After an alert

  • Receipt. You get a receipt that lists each step and its time.
  • New password. After any alert you must choose a new password before anything else.
  • Several payments. If more than one payment is waiting, a notice tells you how many.

  2. Press Reset demo state, choose SIM swap and Rs. 200,000, then press Trigger case.

Drills (Simulate page)

The Simulate page runs emergencies against the real agents: both models down, SMS and email failing, a fraud spike in a district, an agent crash, a silent customer and a manual block switch. In each case the rules still decide and a held payment stays held.

8Share the demo on the internet

To let people try the customer site on their own phones, publish your computer's site through a tunnel. Everything still runs on your computer.

The link only works while your computer is onThe server is your own computer. The link works only while the computer, Docker and the tunnel are running. A quick tunnel gives a new address each time it starts, so an old link stops working. (A named Cloudflare tunnel on a domain you own keeps one address, but the computer still has to be on.)

Each time

  1. Open Docker Desktop and wait for Engine running.
  2. In .env make sure COOKIE_SECURE=true and DEMO_MODE=hybrid are set.
  3. Start the app and the front door:
cd paysheildlk-university/university-project
docker compose --profile share up --build -d
  1. Check http://localhost:8080 opens the site on your computer.
  2. In a second window, start the tunnel (install it once with winget install --id Cloudflare.cloudflared):
cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8080
  1. Wait for Registered tunnel connection. The address in the box (an https://…trycloudflare.com link) is your link.
  2. Share that address followed by /c. Test it on a phone using mobile data before anyone else uses it.

Rules for a safe demo

  • Share only /c. The fraud desk console is on the same address, so keep its login private.
  • Volunteers. In the console, use Add customer with made-up NIC and account numbers. The volunteer then registers on the phone site with exactly those details.
  • Consent. Tell volunteers that their face scan is stored, encrypted, on your computer.
  • Shared database. Everyone shares one demo database. Press Reset demo state between people.
  • Keep the computer awake and turn off sleep. When you finish, press Ctrl+C in the tunnel window and run docker compose --profile share down.

9Real SMS and email

The alert reaches the customer by in-app message, SMS and email. By default, SMS and email are written to the log only. Turn on the real drivers when you are ready.

ChannelSetting in .envNotes
SMS (Text.lk)TEXTLK_API_KEY and the sender settingsYour Text.lk account needs credit. Delivery is not instant.
Email (SMTP)SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, user, password and senderAny SMTP provider works.
Recipient safetyDEMO_ALLOWED_PHONES and DEMO_ALLOWED_EMAILSIn demo mode, real messages go only to numbers and addresses on these lists.

Setting it up

  1. Fill in the key or SMTP settings in .env.
  2. Add the test recipients to DEMO_ALLOWED_PHONES (international format, for example +947…) and DEMO_ALLOWED_EMAILS.
  3. Restart the backend: docker compose up -d.
  4. In the console, use Add customer to create a test customer with the recipient's own phone and email. Register that customer on the phone site.
  5. Trigger a case for that customer. The SMS and email arrive on the registered number and address.

What the messages say

The alert is generic: it asks whether you made a payment and points you to the app. It never contains a link or a code. A message with a link or a code is refused by the system itself, and a per-hour sending budget applies.

Only message people who agreedAdd only your own numbers and addresses, or those of people who have agreed to receive test messages. Anyone not on the allow-list receives nothing.
ReceiptsNo delivery-receipt callback from the providers is integrated, so the system records "sent" or "failed" but never claims a message was "delivered".

10Models and fallbacks

Language models write the short analyst notes. They never choose to hold, block or release.

1 · MetaMain model
2 · Grok (xAI)Fallback
3 · Rules writerAlways available
SettingMeaning
META_API_KEYTurns on the main model (META_MODEL, default muse-ember-1.0).
XAI_API_KEYTurns on the fallback model (XAI_MODEL, default grok-4.20-0309-non-reasoning).
No key setThe built-in rules writer is used for every note.

If the main model fails for any reason (rate limit, timeout or an error), the same request goes to the fallback. If both fail, the rules writer writes the note, so a case never waits on a model.

Check which model was used

  • Health check: http://localhost:8000/api/health lists the main model and the fallback and whether each key is configured.
  • Agents & models page and the live log show the model name on each note.
  • To test the fallback, set META_API_KEY= to empty and restart. Notes should then name the Grok model.
Privacy before modelsEverything sent to a model is first masked. Raw phone numbers, NIC numbers, email addresses and account numbers never reach it, and it never sees security answers or face images.

11Security and privacy

The pilot is built so that a mistake or a manipulated message cannot move money on its own.

ControlHow it works
Sign-inUsername and an Argon2id password hash, then a 6-digit authenticator code. A used code cannot be replayed. Lockouts and per-username and per-address limits apply.
SessionsHttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies that page scripts cannot read. Signing out revokes the tokens, so an earlier copy stops working at once.
EncryptionAuthenticator secrets and face data with AES-256-GCM. Email, phone, NIC and account number with AES-256-GCM-SIV so lookups still work. A wrong key stops the service at start. A key-rotation tool is included.
Agent handoffsHMAC-SHA256 signature, a fresh nonce, an allowed-route table and an expiry. 120 of 120 hostile handoffs were rejected in testing.
Least privilegeEach agent has a written charter. A call outside it is refused (0 occurred in 2,816 benchmark calls).
Face scanA signed, short-lived, one-use challenge. Head turns are measured from the frames, so a recording or still photo fails.
MessagesNo link and no code. Allow-listed recipients in demo mode. A per-hour sending budget.
AuditA hash-chained audit log. Security answers are stored as hashes, and the reply never says which answer was wrong.

What each agent may and may not do

13Troubleshooting

Most problems are one of a few causes. For anything else, collect the last 50 lines of the backend log.

docker compose logs backend --tail 50
SymptomLikely cause and fix
Docker says it cannot connect to the engineDocker Desktop is not running. Open it and wait for Engine running.
The backend stops at start-up with a key errorYour ENCRYPTION_KEY no longer matches the stored data. Restore the old key, or start fresh with docker compose down -v.
Demo controls shows an error instead of the registration detailsUsually an old database with a new key (see above). Reset the volume.
Registration says the details do not matchOne field differs from the bank's record. Copy each value exactly from Demo controls. Your own email or phone will not match a demo customer.
The camera will not startThe page is not on localhost or https, or camera access was blocked. Allow the camera in the browser.
The face scan is not acceptedImprove the light, keep your whole face in the oval, and turn a little further. Poor quality does not count against you.
The authenticator code is rejectedYour phone's clock is wrong. Set the time automatically. Use the code for the correct account entry.
After "Yes" there is no face scan, only questionsNo usable face is on file for that account, so the scan is skipped and four rounds of questions decide. Register the customer again with the face scan to test the full flow.
The phone cannot sign in through the tunnelSet COOKIE_SECURE=true, restart, then sign out and try again.
The shared link does not openThe tunnel window was closed, the computer slept, or Docker stopped. Start the tunnel again and share the new address.
Live activity looks emptyAfter a reset it shows "No demo case yet". Trigger a case, or use Watch it live.
The AI notes say "offline rules engine"No model key is set. Add META_API_KEY or XAI_API_KEY and restart. The decisions are not affected.
A test fails when run together with anotherRun the suites one at a time.

14Technology and limits

Architecture

BrowserConsole + phone site
Next.js proxySame-origin /backend
FastAPI + LangGraphTen agents, rule layer
Postgres + RedisData, checkpoints, limits
LayerTechnology
OrchestrationLangGraph supervisor graph. The case pauses at an interrupt() while the customer answers and resumes from a Postgres checkpoint.
BackendFastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic migrations, Argon2id, TOTP, HMAC-signed handoffs.
Face verificationdlib face embeddings with a signed one-use challenge.
ModelsMeta, then Grok, then a built-in rules writer.
FrontendNext.js 15, React 18, TypeScript, a phone-first customer site.
DeliveryDocker Compose, optional Caddy front door and Cloudflare tunnel for sharing.

Limits you should know about

  • Synthetic data. Results come from invented customers and simulated feeds. They are not real fraud rates.
  • Simulated operator. The telecom feed has the shape of the GSMA CAMARA SIM Swap API. Connecting a real operator means changing TELCO_BASE_URL and agreeing terms. A SIM freeze is recorded as a request; nothing is sent to an operator.
  • False holds. About one in four legitimate payments in the benchmark is held and then released after the customer's check. The threshold can be tuned on the Calibration page.
  • Face matching is demonstration grade. Light, camera and pose affect it, and it does not replace the security questions.
  • One shared demo database. Use Reset demo state between audiences.
  • Not a certified product. There has been no third-party penetration test and no regulator approval.

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI decide whether my money is released?
No. A fixed rule layer decides. The models only write the analyst's note.

What if the models or the telecom feed are down?
The rules still decide, and a held payment stays held.

Can I try it with no internet?
Yes, on localhost. Only the optional AI models, SMS and email need the internet.

Is any real customer data used?
No. Every customer, balance and payment is fictional.
